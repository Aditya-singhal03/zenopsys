# Outreach & Sales Templates

Replace `[brackets]`. Keep every message short. Owners read on their phones between jobs.

## 1. Warm-intro ask (to your own network)

> Hey [Name], quick favor. I'm starting to help small business owners automate their back-office work with AI (things like email processing, quotes, data entry). I'm trying to talk to a few **[US trucking company / motel / insurance agency] owners** just to understand how they run things, not to sell. Do you know anyone who might give me 20 minutes? Happy to return the favor anytime.

## 2. Discovery call script ("Mom Test": ask about their life, not your idea)

Goal: learn, don't pitch. Talk 20% of the time, listen 80%.

1. "Walk me through a typical day/week for you and your team."
2. "What takes up the most time that you wish someone else handled?"
3. "Tell me about the last time [task] went wrong. What did it cost you?"
4. "How are you handling it today? Software, a person, spreadsheets?"
5. "Have you tried to fix it? What happened?" *(If they never tried, the pain may not be real.)*
6. "Roughly how many [emails/calls/quotes/documents] a week? How long does each one take?"
7. "Who besides you would be involved in deciding to fix this?"
8. *(Only at the end)* "If I could take [task] off your team's plate in a couple of weeks, is that something you'd pay for this month?"

Write down: exact phrases they use (reuse them in your copy), volume numbers, tools, and who decides.

## 3. Mystery-shop Loom outreach

**Before recording:** call them after hours, submit their web form, or email for a quote. Note the response time and what happened.

**Loom script (60–90 seconds)**
> Hi [Name], I'm [You]. I help [industry] businesses respond to customers faster using AI.
> Last [Tuesday at 6:40pm] I [called / filled out your quote form] as a potential customer. [Nobody answered and voicemail was full / I got a reply 31 hours later.]
> For a [roofing] company, that's probably [$X jobs] a month going to whoever answers first.
> Here's a quick demo of what that would look like with [instant text-back + AI that books the appointment into Jobber]. *(show 20 seconds of the demo)*
> I'd set it up in about two weeks, and if it doesn't book you more jobs in the first month, you don't pay the monthly fee. Worth a 15-minute call?

**Email to carry the Loom**
> Subject: called [Company] last Tuesday
>
> Hi [Name], I called [Company] after hours last week as a customer, and recorded a 90-sec video of what happened and a quick fix: [Loom link]
>
> Worth 15 minutes?
> [You]

## 4. Cold email sequence (US, back-office niche example: freight brokers)

**Email 1**
> Subject: quote emails at [Company]
>
> Hi [Name], [personalized line, e.g. "saw you're hiring a second ops coordinator" / "noticed you run reefer lanes out of Texas"].
>
> Most brokers I talk to have someone spending 3–4 hours a day reading load-tender emails and re-typing them into the TMS before they can even quote.
>
> I build AI that reads those emails (and attached PDFs), drafts the quote, and puts the load in [their TMS, e.g. Tai / McLeod / Aljex]. Your team just reviews and clicks approve.
>
> Open to a 2-minute video showing it on sample tenders?
>
> [You]
> [One-page site] · [US phone]

**Email 2 (3 days later)**
> Hi [Name], here's that 2-min demo: [Loom]. 20 tender emails → structured quotes → pushed to the TMS, with a human approval step. Want me to run it on 10 of your real (anonymized) emails? Free, takes 48 hrs.

**Email 3 (4 days later)**
> Hi [Name], is speed-to-quote a priority at [Company] right now, or is something else bigger this quarter (carrier vetting, check calls, invoicing)? Happy to point you to something useful either way.

**Email 4 (breakup, 7 days later)**
> I'll stop here, [Name]. If manual tender entry ever becomes a headache, reply "tenders" and I'll send the demo over.

Include in the footer: your physical address and "Reply 'no' and I won't email again." (CAN-SPAM)

## 9. Proposal skeleton (1 page)

1. **What we heard:** their words, their numbers ("Your 2 coordinators spend ~25 hrs/week on tender entry").
2. **What we'll build:** one workflow, with the human approval step stated clearly.
3. **What success looks like:** a measurable target (hours saved, response time, jobs booked) and how we'll measure it.
4. **Timeline:** 2–4 weeks with weekly demos.
5. **Investment:** setup fee + monthly retainer + guarantee.
6. **Data & security:** where data lives, access, NDA, deletion on exit.
7. **Next step:** sign + 50% upfront → kickoff call date.
